# Project Shattered World - Decision Log

## Core Game

- Single player only
- Mobile first
- Portrait orientation
- Pixel-art graphics
- Zelda-style movement
- Auto attack combat

## Progression

- Level cap 50 at launch
- Legacy/Paragon system after max level
- Skill trees are a major progression system
- Skills gain experience through use
- Progression priority:
  Gear > Skills > Levels > Legacy

## Combat

- Auto attack by default
- Approximately 6 skill slots
- 2-4 skills can be set to autocast
- Manual movement is the primary defensive mechanic
- No dodge roll button
- Health regenerates outside combat

## Skills

Launch archetypes:

- Fire
- Lightning
- Summoning
- Melee

Hybrid trees unlock later.

## Loot

Item rarity:

- Rare
- Epic
- Legendary

Legendary items should:

- Change builds
- Change skills
- Feel memorable

Legendary items should NOT simply provide larger numbers.

## World

- Post-catastrophe setting
- Human settlements are rare
- Exploration is a major pillar
- Secrets are hidden throughout the world
- Rumors guide exploration

## Story

- Main theme: Curiosity
- Catastrophe is tied to a broken seal
- Demons were once imprisoned
- Early game focuses on corrupted animals and humans
- True demons appear much later
- The full truth is discovered gradually

## Bestiary

- Bestiary provides lore
- Bestiary provides small permanent rewards
- Rewards must never become mandatory

## Endgame

- Difficult dungeons
- Powerful bosses
- Secret encounters
- Long-term character progression
- Build optimization

## Hard Rules

Never add:

- Daily quests
- Weekly quests
- Energy systems
- Forced login rewards
- Pay-to-win systems
- Excessive RNG progression
- Full elemental immunities
